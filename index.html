What the tools do

Cut

Mandalas, signs and spray stencils with automatic bridges and island checks. Exports SVG for lasers and DXF for routers and plasma tables.

Relief

Print

Turns signs and stencil designs into 3D-printable models.

Pavement

Lane arrows, bike symbols and parking-lot lettering at full size, split into overlapping panels with registration holes.
